# Article template and pre-publish checklist

Use this for every article on the site. The front matter is for your records; the checklist is what matters.

```yaml
title: "<Specific headline that names the entity: drug, agency, program, number>"
slug: <lowercase-hyphenated-headline-5-to-9-words>
meta_description: "<140–155 characters: what happened + what it means for patients>"
author: "Medhane Hagos Mesgena, MD"
date: YYYY-MM-DD
cluster: <peptides | vaccines | medicare | screening | equity-access>
primary_query: "<the search phrase a patient would type>"
internal_links:
  - <url of a related article on your site>
  - <url of a related article on your site>
sources:
  - <primary source url: FDA, HHS, CMS, CDC, Federal Register>
  - <reputable secondary source>
```

## Structure that works (600–900 words)

1. **Dek / first paragraph:** what happened, when, who decided it. One short paragraph.
2. **What it actually says:** the concrete details (names, doses, dates, dollar amounts). Bullets are fine.
3. **What it does not say:** the caveat the headlines skip (e.g. "this is not an approval").
4. **What I tell patients:** the family-physician perspective. Three to five practical points.
5. **What to watch next:** the next date or decision.
6. **Author box:** "Medhane Hagos Mesgena, MD, is a board-certified family physician who writes about preventive care,
   health equity and healthcare policy. More at <primary site link>."

## Pre-publish checklist

- [ ] Title contains the named entity and is under 65 characters.
- [ ] Meta description written (not auto-truncated body text).
- [ ] Byline reads exactly `Medhane Hagos Mesgena, MD`.
- [ ] Author box present and links to the hub/primary site.
- [ ] At least two internal links to your own articles; at least three outbound links to primary sources.
- [ ] One image with descriptive alt text (no stock image of pills if avoidable; a chart or document screenshot is better).
- [ ] Clinical statements reviewed by you; nothing stated beyond what the source says.
- [ ] Published, then: URL added to sitemap, Request indexing in Search Console, Bing URL submission, LinkedIn post.
