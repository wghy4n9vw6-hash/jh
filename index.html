Medhane Hagos Mesgena, MD

Board-certified family physician · Writer on preventive care, early detection and health equity

Medhane Hagos Mesgena is a board-certified family physician. His published commentary focuses on preventive care and health screenings, on designing healthcare that reaches communities before a crisis, and on the infrastructure, telemedicine and training needed to bring care to underserved and rural populations.

His current writing appears on his article site, Medhane Hagos Mesgena — Articles, where he covers health policy and clinical news from a primary-care perspective.
